Let's walk through how Hermes actually works when you give it a task. Step one: Understand Task. Hermes parses your request and identifies the goals. Step two: Plan Steps. It breaks down the task into executable actions. Step three: Execute Tools. This is where the real work happens — running code, searching the web, reading files, calling APIs. Step four: Learn and Remember. After completing the task, Hermes saves successful patterns to memory. And here's the key — this isn't a one-time process. There's a continuous improvement loop. Every task makes Hermes smarter for the next one.
